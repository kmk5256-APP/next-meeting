Next Meeting

An iPhone app for finding A.A. meetings by ZIP code: what's on now, what starts soon, and how to get there.

Get in touch

Questions, bug reports and ideas go through GitHub. Open an issue and I'll reply there.

Open an issue

Issues are public. Please don't include your name, where you go to meetings, or anything else personal. Describe the problem and the ZIP code you searched, and that's plenty.

A meeting's details are wrong

Listings come straight from the local A.A. office that publishes them, and the app shows them as they are. The office is the one that can fix a time, address or link. In the app, open the meeting and tap Report a correction. That writes to the office directly.

My area isn't covered

The app reads meeting lists that local offices publish openly. Some offices choose not to share theirs that way, and the app respects that. You can find any local office through A.A.'s own directory. If your office publishes an open list and it isn't in the app yet, open an issue with a link to the office's website.

Reminders don't show the meeting's name

That's on purpose. Reminders are discreet by default, so a glance at your lock screen shows only a time. You can change this under Help, then Privacy, in the app.

Where is my data?

On your phone. There's no account and no server. The privacy policy has the details.

If you need to talk to someone now

  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: call or text 988, any time.
  • SAMHSA National Helpline: 1-800-662-4357, free, confidential, 24 hours a day.
  • In an emergency, call 911.
