Privacy policy

Going to a meeting is your business. Next Meeting is built so that it stays that way.

Effective November 17, 2026.

The short version

Next Meeting does not collect, store or share any personal information. There's no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The app has no server of its own. What you save stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

  • Saved meetings, your notes on them, and your reminders. Stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Recent ZIP codes. Kept on your device so you can tap them again. You can clear them in the app under Help.
  • Downloaded meeting lists. Cached on your device so the app works with a weak connection. You can clear them in the app under Help.

What leaves your phone, and where it goes

  • ZIP codes you search. Sent to Apple's mapping service to find where the ZIP code is. Apple's handling of that request is covered by Apple's privacy policy.
  • Your location, only if you tap "Use my location". Used once for that search, with Apple's mapping service turning it into a place name. It is not stored and not added to your recent searches.
  • Requests for meeting lists. The app downloads meeting lists directly from the websites of the local A.A. offices that publish them. Those websites receive an ordinary web request, which includes your IP address, as any website visit does. They are not told your ZIP code, your location, or which meetings you open or save.
  • Links you choose to open. Directions, online meeting links, phone numbers, office websites and contribution links open in the app you'd expect (Maps, Zoom, Phone, Safari and so on). What happens there is governed by that service.

Calendar and notifications

Adding a meeting to your calendar opens Apple's own event screen. You review and save it yourself, and the app cannot read your calendar. Reminders are scheduled on your device by iOS. Nothing about them is sent anywhere.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Because the app collects nothing, a change would only ever describe new features, not new uses of your data.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the project's GitHub page. Issues there are public, so please keep personal details out of them.

Next Meeting is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Alcoholics Anonymous World Services, Inc. or any A.A. group or office.
